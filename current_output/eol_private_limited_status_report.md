## 9. OBSERVATION

The commercial invoice, letter of credit and Certificate 096 identify a shipment valued at USD 130,780.00. The certificate records NPR 21,463,613.60 including 10% incidental value at NPR 149.20 per USD. Table 3 contains eight water-heater models and one free-of-charge spare-parts line; model quantities and prices obscured in the commercial-invoice OCR are corroborated by field 45A of the letter of credit and the proforma invoice. Spare parts occupy five cartons; their individual piece count is not stated.

The letter dated 2026/08/26 confirms deputation on that date and refers to survey observations, but gives no separate survey date, place, itemized damage findings or estimated amount. The claim number, formal intimation, customs clearance particulars and delivery chronology are also absent from the available OCR. IRD registration fields remain blank because no verified IRD lookup is supplied.

The reported loss date of 2026/08/20 follows the stated declaration expiry of 01/07/2026. Shipment was recorded as on board on 09/06/2026, while the certificate was issued on 07/04/2026. The transit and delivery chronology and applicable duration of cover therefore require verification. The certificate lists Inland Transit Clauses C (Fire Risk), whereas the policy schedule lists Inland Transit Clauses A; the original applicable clauses require reconciliation. Cumulative sums insured vary with dated amendments and are distinguished from the shipment’s declared value in Table 1. The selected endorsement is the 07/04/2026 amendment matching the certificate’s cumulative sum insured; later amendment references include noisy OCR. These matters remain open for documentary verification, and this preliminary report makes no determination of liability.
