## Adequacy And Assessment Notes

The example records construction machinery sum insured as NRs. 1,000,000.00. The report states that underinsurance was not applied because the assessed loss was less than the sum insured allocated for construction machinery. The assessed amount shown in the adequacy table is NRs. 245,195.57.

## Observation

The report concludes that the claimed loss was verified through survey, document review, stock list comparison, photographs, and policy coverage review. It records that depreciation was not applied because the machinery age was less than one year and some machinery was on lease. Salvage was not applied because machinery was swept away or buried and could not be further used. A minimum policy excess of NRs. 10,000 was deducted as per policy guidelines.
