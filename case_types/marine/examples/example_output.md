### BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF DAMAGE SITES & DETAIL OF LOSS

As per insured, damage have occurred to electronic and electrical goods during transportation from India, New Delhi to Kathmandu, Nepal via Bhairahawa customs office. Upon the arrival of consignment at warehouse of the Him Electronics Pvt. Ltd., the insured commenced unloading the packages. During the process, it was observed that several cartons were visibly torn and damaged. Recognizing the potential for internal damage, the insured opened a selection of these cartons and confirmed that the electronic and electrical goods were affected.

Following the discovery, the insured requested a formal and detailed inspection to determine the full extent of loss. During field observation, we confirmed that several cartons were visibly torn and damaged. Upon opening the damaged packaging for a detailed inspection, it was confirmed that out of a total consignment quantity of 2,276 units, a total of 27 units of electrical goods including amplifier, portable set, wall speaker and microphone were found to be damaged. Specifically, 4 units of goods were found to be highly damaged and cracked, while remaining 23 units of goods sustained minor scratches and dents.
