## 3. Cause Of The Loss

According to the intimation dated 05 May 2026, Ojas Construction and Interior Pvt. Ltd. reported that a neighboring owner, Sarita Manandhar, whose house is situated on the east side of the client site, complained of property damage and filed a case with the municipality seeking repair and reimbursement.

The Kathmandu Metropolitan City ward/legal department report records that the construction work involved piling for protection, raft foundation and shear wall works. The report further states that settlement caused by piling and foundation construction may have resulted in the observed cracks and deflection in the neighboring house. ## 5. Brief Description Of Damaged Site And Details Of The Loss

The available OCR does not show a separate field survey date by the appointed surveyor. However, the Kathmandu Metropolitan City report records a site inspection relating to the complaint at KMC Ward No. 13.

The municipal report notes visible cracks at the apron and boundary wall, wall and beam joint cracks at the first floor south-east cantilever projection, hairline cracks on top floor walls, beam, column and stair cover, and westward deflection measured from the western parapet. It records deflection of 88mm at the north-west wall and 100mm at the south-west wall toward the west side from the plumb line. The report recommends structural engineer opinion to determine whether the deflection is within ultimate and serviceability limit states and to advise crack remedies.
